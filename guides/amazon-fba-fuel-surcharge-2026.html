Amazon FBA 3.5% fuel surcharge is on the fulfillment fee, not the sale price.

Standalone explainer. Official Amazon announcement only. Compiled 23 Aug 2026. Confirm billed amounts in Seller Central. Full FBA walkthrough: Amazon FBA calculator (2026).

Short answer. Since 17 April 2026, US (and Canada) FBA fulfillment fees are multiplied by 1.035: surcharge = fulfillment fee × 3.5%. Amazon’s announcement said that averages about $0.17 per unit for US FBA, varying by size and dimensions. It is not 3.5% of the item price, and it is not a referral or storage fee.

What the official notice said

Amazon’s Seller Central announcement (posted publicly by News_Amazon) titled Fuel and logistics-related surcharge: FBA, MCF, and BWP in US and CA states:

  • 17 Apr 2026: 3.5% on fulfillment fees for FBA in the US and Canada, and Remote Fulfillment with FBA from the US into Canada, Mexico, and Brazil.
  • 2 May 2026: the same surcharge on Buy with Prime (US) and Multi-Channel Fulfillment (US and Canada).
  • Calculated on fulfillment fees, not the sale price of the items.
  • On average, $0.17 per unit for US FBA, varying by item size and dimensions.
  • Revenue Calculator, Profit Analytics, and Fee and Economics Preview were updated to show the surcharge.

That $0.17 figure is Amazon’s stated average, not your SKU. A small-standard unit with a low fulfillment fee pays less than $0.17; a heavier or larger unit pays more. 3.5% of a $24.99 sale would be about $0.87 — that is the wrong base.
