How Etsy Offsite Ads fees work (15%, 12%, $10k lock-in, $100 cap)

Standalone explainer. Official Etsy citations only. Last checked against policy text 23 Aug 2026. Confirm on your Etsy Payments statement.

Short answer. Offsite Ads is a separate fee on attributed orders only. It is 15% while your shop has never reached $10,000 USD in any trailing 365-day window (you can opt out). It becomes 12% for the lifetime of the shop the first time you hit that $10k threshold. The fee on any one order is capped at $100 USD. Attribution uses a 30-day click window. It does not replace the 6.5% transaction fee or Etsy Payments processing — those still apply on the same order.

When the fee applies

Etsy charges Offsite Ads only when an order is attributed to an Offsite Ad: a buyer clicks the ad and purchases within 30 days. It is not a shop-wide take rate and it is not blended into the 6.5% transaction line. If the order is not attributed, the Offsite Ads fee is $0.

Which rate: 15% or 12%

  • 15% — the shop has always been under $10,000 USD in any consecutive 365-day period. Participation can be opted out.
  • 12% — the shop has made $10,000 USD or more in any consecutive 365-day period. The 12% rate then applies for the lifetime of the shop, including later periods under $10k. Participation becomes required.

That $10k lock-in is why a shop that once crossed the threshold does not go back to 15% or to opt-out.

$100 per-order cap

The Offsite Ads fee on any one order will not exceed $100 USD. A large attributed order is still 15% or 12% of the order amount until that product hits $100, then it stops.

What the percentage is taken on

Official policy uses the order amount: the price you display plus shipping and gift wrapping (and, in some jurisdictions, taxes). Sales tax is not in the US transaction-fee base; treat tax on Offsite Ads as jurisdiction-specific and confirm on the statement.

FeeTruth models the Offsite Ads base the same way as the US transaction base: item × quantity + shipping + gift wrap (shipping and gift wrap are order-level, not multiplied by quantity). Tax is not added to that Offsite Ads base in the calculator.

It does not replace 6.5% + processing

Offsite Ads is an extra line. An attributed order still pays:

  1. Listing fee ($0.20 USD per listed or renewed unit), if applicable
  2. Transaction fee — 6.5% of displayed price + shipping + gift wrap (US: not on sales tax)
  3. Etsy Payments processing — US 3% + $0.25 on the gross order amount including shipping and tax
  4. Offsite Ads — 15% or 12% of the order amount, capped at $100

Do not fold Offsite Ads into a single “Etsy take rate” with the 6.5% and processing lines. They are separate official fees.

Worked example: $50 item + $8 shipping

US shop, quantity 1, no gift wrap, no tax, one attributed order. Order amount for transaction and Offsite Ads = $50 + $8 = $58.00.

The 6.5% + processing subtotal is $5.76 either way ($3.77 + $1.99). Offsite Ads is the additional $8.70 or $6.96 — not a substitute for those two lines. Neither rate hits the $100 cap on a $58 order (the cap would bind around a $667 order at 15%, or about $834 at 12%).
