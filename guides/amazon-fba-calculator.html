Short answer. The 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge (since 17 Apr 2026) multiplies the fulfillment fee, not the sale price.

Fulfillment dollars are estimates until you paste Fee Preview

Public Amazon pages describe the method (size + weight; Low-Price FBA under $10) but do not publish the 2026 dollar grid. FeeTruth’s size/weight bands are a conservative transcription of publicly circulated 2026 non-peak US FBA rates, labeled estimate.

Size-tier cutoffs we ship (also estimate — long-standing public US FBA rules; confirm in Seller Central):

  • Small standard: longest ≤ 15 in, median ≤ 12 in, shortest ≤ 0.75 in, unit weight ≤ 1 lb. Shipping weight = unit weight.
  • Large standard: longest ≤ 18 in, median ≤ 14 in, shortest ≤ 8 in, shipping weight ≤ 20 lb. Shipping weight = greater of unit weight and DIM (L × W × H / 139).
  • Oversize / bulky: detailed extra-large card is not modeled. Use a Seller Central override.

When you have Revenue Calculator / Fee Preview, paste that fulfillment fee in the override field. That line becomes override, and the 3.5% surcharge is 3.5% of the pasted number — not of our estimate.

3.5% fuel surcharge is on fulfillment, not the sale price

Since 17 April 2026, US (and Canada) FBA fulfillment fees are multiplied by 1.035. Amazon’s announcement said that averages about $0.17 per unit for US FBA, varying by size and dimensions. It is not 3.5% of the item price, and it is not a referral or storage fee. Buy with Prime (US) and Multi-Channel Fulfillment (US and Canada) picked up the same surcharge on 2 May 2026.

3.5% of a $24.99 sale would be about $0.87. That is the wrong base. The longer write-up is Amazon FBA 3.5% fuel surcharge (2026).

Questions this calculator is built to answer

How do I calculate Amazon FBA fees in 2026?

Add the official referral (and plan / media closing if they apply), then add fulfillment + 3.5% of that fulfillment fee. Use Seller Central Fee Preview for the fulfillment dollar. Size-weight calculators that skip the override are estimates.

Is the 3.5% fuel surcharge on the sale price?

No. Official announcement: calculated on fulfillment fees, not the sale price of the items.

Did Amazon change referral percentages in 2026?

The public sell.amazon.com/pricing table we fetched on 22 Aug 2026 still showed the long-standing category percentages. The 2026 letter we cite is about FBA fulfillment averages, not a new public referral-rate rewrite.

Why does FeeTruth refuse to give one FBA take-rate?

Referral is a category percent of price. Fulfillment is a size/weight/price-band dollar. Fuel is 3.5% of fulfillment. Storage is cubic-foot. Blending them hides which line is official and which line is a guess.
