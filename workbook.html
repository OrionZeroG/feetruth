How to use

  1. Open the CSV in Google Sheets, LibreOffice, or Excel (File → Import).
  2. One row per SKU / listing. Keep marketplace and currency in the dedicated columns.
  3. Paste official fees from Etsy Payment account or Amazon Fee Preview when they differ from FeeTruth.
  4. Do not put passwords, API keys, or customer PII in this file.
