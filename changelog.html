15 Jan 2026 US Referral and FBA updates take effect. Amazon said FBA fees rise by an average of $0.08 per unit (under 0.5% of an average item’s selling price). Letter says no new FBA fee types in 2026. Seller-forum posts repeating the letter: standard-size under $10 about +$0.05 avg; $10–$50 about +$0.08 avg; above $50 about +$0.31 avg; small-standard in the $10–$50 band about +$0.25. Etsy

WhenWhat we can citeCite
Still in force 23 Aug 2026 Listing $0.20 USD per listed/renewed item. Transaction 6.5% on displayed listing price + shipping + gift wrap (US: not on sales tax). Quantity multiplies the listing price, not the shipping / gift wrap / tax charged on the order. Official text: “the price you display for each listing plus the amount you charge for shipping and gift wrapping.” These rates appear on the current Fees & Payments Policy. We did not find an official 2026 announcement changing the 6.5% transaction rate (that rate has been 6.5% since April 2022 per Etsy’s earlier public materials). etsy.com/legal/fees
FeeTruth engine correction 23 Aug 2026 Earlier builds multiplied shipping, gift wrap, and tax by quantity. That overstated fees when qty>1 and those fields were nonzero (example: qty=2, $48 item + $6.50 ship + $5 tax, ads off — engine had been $11.305 vs official-base $10.5375). Processing remains “gross order amount, including shipping and tax” + the official per-order flat fee. Offsite Ads still use the same order base and $100 cap. legal/fees · legal/etsy-payments
Still in force 22 Aug 2026 Etsy Payments processing still listed as US 3% + $0.25 and UK 4% + £0.20 on the official country table. etsy.com/legal/etsy-payments
Still in force 22 Aug 2026 Offsite Ads: 15% under $10,000 USD in any trailing 365-day window; 12% once that threshold is ever met (lifetime). Per-order cap $100 USD. legal/fees · Offsite Ads help
No 2026 public rate change found Pattern remains $15 USD/mo after trial. Etsy Plus remains $10 USD/mo. We did not find an official 2026 price change for either. legal/fees · Pattern / Plus help articles
